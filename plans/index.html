FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does The Predictive Index cost?
PI plans start at $8,500/year and are priced by headcount, beginning at 50 employees. Your price scales with your company size and the modules you choose. All plans include unlimited users and assessments — there are no per-seat fees.
What's included in each PI plan?
Every plan includes unlimited behavioral assessments, candidate-to-job matching, and personalized interview guides. The Hire + Inspire plan adds manager coaching and relationship insights. The Professional tier adds team design and strategy tools. The Full Platform includes everything — engagement surveys, diagnostics, and performance management.
Can I change plans later?
Yes. You can upgrade your plan at any time, and our team will help you transition smoothly. Many customers start with Hire + Inspire and expand as their behavioral insights practice matures.
What's the difference between Hire + Inspire and the Full Platform?
Hire + Inspire focuses on making better hiring decisions and giving managers coaching tools. The Full Platform adds team design analytics, engagement surveys, organizational diagnostics, and performance management — everything you need for behavioral insights across the entire employee lifecycle.
Do I need to pay per user or per assessment?
No. All PI plans include unlimited users and unlimited assessments. Your price is based on company size and the modules you choose, not usage.
How does pricing work for larger companies?
PI pricing is based on headcount, starting at 50 employees, and scales as your organization grows. Use the company-size selector near the top of the page to see starting prices for your band. For organizations over 400 employees, we'll tailor a quote to your specific needs.
Do you offer training or workshops?
Yes. Every plan includes onboarding and training. For teams that want to go deeper, PI also offers expert-led workshops — including The Predictive Index Professional Certification, a facilitated 5-module series for HR leaders and executives. It's an optional way to build a clear game plan and apply behavioral insights across the entire employee lifecycle, delivered in person or virtually.
