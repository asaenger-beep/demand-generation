Guess the Profile

A behavioral spin on the classic board game. We've secretly picked one of the 17 PI Reference Profiles. Type your own yes-or-no questions, flip down the profiles you've ruled out, and lock in your guess when you're ready.

How to play
  1. Ask your own questions. Type any yes/no question about how the mystery person is wired — the profile answers honestly based on its behavioral drives (Dominance, Extraversion, Patience, Formality). Stuck for words? Browse the question bank for ideas.
  2. Narrow it down. Click any profile on the board to flip it face-down once you've ruled it out — just like knocking down the tiles in the real game.
  3. Make your guess. When you think you know who it is, choose them in the guess box and lock it in. Guess right and you win; guess wrong and that tile flips down so you can keep going. Solve it in as few moves as possible to beat your best score!
